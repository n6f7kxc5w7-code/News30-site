Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

News30 is a free site that turns news reporting into short AI-condensed video summaries. It is run by an individual based in Norway, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. The EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. Questions or requests: aidrusomar2009@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Without an account. You can watch stories without signing in. We do not store a profile or activity history for visitors who are not signed in.
  • Account details. If you sign in (with Google or with email and password), sign-in is handled by our database provider, Supabase. We receive and store your email address and a user ID, and Google sign-in may also share your name.
  • Your saved progress. While signed in, your profile and activity on News30 are saved to your account so they work across devices: saved stories, likes, quiz results and login streaks.
  • Usage events. While signed in, we also record events such as watching a video, liking or saving a story, completing a quiz and logging in. Each event is stored in Supabase with your user ID, your email address and a timestamp.
  • Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies, and it reports aggregated statistics rather than identifying individual visitors.
  • Ask AI. If you use Ask AI, the question you type, along with the story it is about, is sent to Google Gemini or DeepSeek to generate an answer. Don't include personal or sensitive information in your questions. These providers process the text under their own terms, and DeepSeek's servers are located outside the EEA.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to run and protect the service.

How we use it

We use this data only to run News30: to sign you in, keep your saved stories and progress, answer Ask AI questions, and understand which features and stories are used so we can improve the site. The legal bases are performing the service you asked for (your account and its features) and our legitimate interest in running and improving a small free service (analytics and logs).

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and the database.
  • Vercel: hosting, server logs and Web Analytics.
  • Google (Gemini) and DeepSeek: generating Ask AI answers.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose “Sign in with Google”.

Some of these providers process data outside Norway and the EEA. Where that happens, the transfer relies on the safeguards the provider offers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, your browser stores a login token so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

How long we keep it

Account data, saved progress and usage events are kept while your account exists. If you ask us to delete them, we will. Hosting logs are kept only briefly, according to Vercel's retention settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and you can object to or ask us to restrict how we process it.

To delete your account and data, email aidrusomar2009@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We will act on your request within 30 days.

You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Children

News30 is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
